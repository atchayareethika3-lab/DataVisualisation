# Automobile Sales Data Visualization

## Project Overview

This project analyzes automobile sales data using Python and data visualization techniques. The objective is to identify trends and patterns in automobile sales during recession and non-recession periods.

The analysis explores the relationship between sales, advertising expenditure, GDP, unemployment rates, vehicle pricing, and seasonality.

---

## Technologies Used

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn

---

## Dataset

The dataset contains information related to:

- Automobile Sales
- Vehicle Type
- Advertising Expenditure
- GDP
- Average Vehicle Price
- Unemployment Rate
- Recession Indicator
- Year and Month

---

## Visualizations Created

### 1. Yearly Automobile Sales Trend
- Line chart showing total automobile sales across different years.

### 2. Advertising Expenditure vs Sales
- Comparison between advertising expenditure and automobile sales during non-recession periods.

### 3. Vehicle Type Sales Comparison
- Bar chart comparing average vehicle sales during recession and non-recession periods.

### 4. GDP Analysis
- Subplots comparing GDP trends during recession and non-recession periods.

### 5. Seasonality Impact
- Bubble plot showing the relationship between month and automobile sales.

### 6. Vehicle Price vs Sales
- Scatter plot analyzing the impact of average vehicle prices on sales during recession periods.

### 7. Advertising Expenditure Distribution
- Pie chart showing advertising expenditure share between recession and non-recession periods.

### 8. Advertising Spend by Vehicle Type
- Pie chart displaying advertising expenditure allocation across vehicle categories during recession.

### 9. Unemployment Rate vs Sales
- Line chart illustrating the impact of unemployment rates on automobile sales by vehicle type.

---

## Key Insights

- Automobile sales fluctuate significantly during recession periods.
- Advertising expenditure shows a strong relationship with sales performance.
- GDP trends influence overall market demand.
- Vehicle pricing and unemployment rates affect consumer purchasing behavior.
- Sales patterns vary across different vehicle categories.
